01 — OVERVIEW

Understanding the Problem

About StudyHub

StudyHub is an educational platform designed to help students discover online courses, learn new skills, and monitor their learning progress in one place.

The goal of this case study is to understand the student's journey and identify opportunities to make the learning experience simpler, faster, and more engaging.

Project StudyHub
Category EdTech
Platform Mobile App
Focus Student Experience
🎯

Objective

Create a simple and motivating learning experience for students.

👥

Target Users

College students and young professionals learning new skills online.

💡

UX Goal

Reduce friction and help users reach their learning goals faster.

03 — UX ANALYSIS

Strengths & Weaknesses

Reviewing the current experience reveals what works well and where users may struggle.

✓

Strengths

What works well
  • ✓ Simple navigation structure
  • ✓ Clear course categories
  • ✓ Visible learning progress
  • ✓ Easy-to-understand course information
  • ✓ Consistent visual hierarchy
!

Weaknesses

Potential pain points
  • ! Course discovery can feel overwhelming
  • ! Limited personalization
  • ! Progress information lacks motivation
  • ! Too many choices for new users
  • ! Checkout/learning flow could be shorter

Key User Pain Points

01

Finding the Right Course

Users may spend too much time comparing multiple courses before choosing one.

02

Losing Motivation

Users may stop learning when progress feels slow or unclear.

03

Information Overload

Too many options can make it difficult for users to decide what to learn next.

04 — UX IMPROVEMENTS

Three Opportunities for Improvement

Based on the journey and pain points, these improvements can create a smoother and more motivating experience.

01
✨

Personalized Dashboard

Recommend courses based on the student's interests, previous learning activity, and progress.

UX Benefit Less searching + more relevant content
02
🔎

Smarter Course Discovery

Add filters for skill level, duration, topic, rating, and learning goal to help users quickly find the right course.

UX Benefit Faster decisions + reduced overload
03
📈

Motivating Progress Tracking

Introduce milestones, achievements, weekly goals, and learning streaks to encourage consistent learning.

UX Benefit More motivation + better retention
